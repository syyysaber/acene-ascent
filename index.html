ASCENT

A stray π-electron fell out of conjugation. Excite it back up a shrinking ladder of aromatic rings before the whole system falls apart.

  • Acene — pentacene → naphthalene, rings shrink as you climb
  • B–N acene — isoelectronic swap, jolts you sky-high
  • B–B acene — naphthalene to a folded diboraheptacene, crumbles in one step
  • Helicene — angularly fused and twisted, drifts sideways
  • S,S-anthracene — stable, doesn't break; its hidden fold just angles the launch
  • B,B + S,S pentacene — both traits at once: crumbles AND angles the launch
  • hν photon — rare pickup, excites you into a sustained climb
  • Carbocation — rare and electron-hungry, pulls you in and quenches your run
